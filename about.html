Our Background & Expertise

Who is MAI

Firm Overview

Founded in 1987, **Messett Associates, Inc. (MAI)** is a leading international executive search firm built upon a commitment to absolute discretion, client-focused strategy, and deep executive sourcing capabilities.

MAI specializes in recruiting senior-level leaders and board directors. Our search projects generally target highly compensated key personnel, typically starting at salaries of $150,000 and above, where matching the perfect combination of corporate cultural alignment, specialized skillsets, and leadership acumen is critical.

Over the decades, we have successfully developed cross-border relationships, executing recruitment programs across the **United States, Latin America, the Caribbean, Europe, and Asia**.

Founder & Managing Partner

William J. Messett III founded MAI in 1987. With over three decades of international headhunting and strategic merchant banking expertise, he has advised scores of multi-national conglomerates on high-value leadership placements, joint ventures, and distribution networks globally.

Practice Profile

MAI operates across a diverse range of industries and disciplines. Our team brings deep functional knowledge, enabling us to locate outstanding candidates who align with our clients' business goals.

Key Industry Sectors

  • Consumer Products & Retail: Food, beverage, luxury goods, and durables.
  • Financial Services: Investment banking, merchant banking, asset management, and commercial banking.
  • Telecommunications & Technology: Hardware, software, digital services, and network infrastructure.
  • Industrial & Manufacturing: Automotive, heavy machinery, chemical, and supply chain.
  • Healthcare & Life Sciences: Pharmaceuticals, medical devices, biotechnology, and healthcare delivery.

Functional Leadership Areas

  • Chief Executives: Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Chief Operating Officer (COO), Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
  • Divisional Presidents: Managing Directors, Country Managers, General Managers.
  • Functional Vice Presidents: VP of Sales, VP of Marketing, VP of Human Resources, VP of Technology.
  • Board Positions: Corporate Directors, Independent Advisors, Non-Executive Board Members.
