Our Services & Methodology

Retained Executive Search

For critical board-level, C-suite, and senior management appointments, companies need a thorough, exhaustive approach. Standard contingent placement models often focus on speed, sending already-active candidates. At MAI, we look deeper.

Our retainer search model allows us to assign a dedicated research team to exhaustively study the market. We contact, pre-qualify, and interview candidates who are currently successful in their roles and not actively looking. This passive talent pool represents the top tier of industry leaders.

Every search is treated with absolute confidentiality, protecting both the client organization's competitive strategies and the candidate's career standing.

Why Choose Retained Search?

Choose MAI's retained executive search for:

  • C-Suite, VP, and Director level roles.
  • Highly specialized technical or functional requirements.
  • Confidential replacements of current executives.
  • Cross-border placements requiring multi-lingual coordination.

The Search Process

We utilize a systematic six-stage recruitment methodology to guarantee success for every search assignment.

01

Initiation & Strategy Formulation

We consult extensively with client leadership to understand the corporate culture, strategic goals, and define the candidate profile specifications (responsibilities, requirements, and compensation parameters).

02

Research & Target Mapping

Our research team conducts comprehensive mapping of competitor and peer organizations. We formulate a target list of firms and map the relevant executives within those targets.

03

Candidate Sourcing & Prequalification

We initiate direct, discreet contact with target prospects. We conduct rigorous screening and face-to-face (or virtual) interviews to evaluate their leadership capabilities, experience, and motivation.

04

Shortlist & Profile Presentation

We present a highly qualified shortlist of candidates accompanied by detailed profiles, evaluation summaries, and verification reports. We then coordinate client-candidate interviews.

05

Reference Audit & Package Negotiation

We conduct in-depth background checks and professional reference audits from former superiors, peers, and subordinates. We assist in structuring and negotiating the final executive compensation package.

06

Onboarding Support & Follow-Up

We assist in the transition process, helping the executive onboard smoothly. We maintain regular contact with both the client and the executive during the first year to ensure long-term success.

Merchant Banking & M&A Advisory

Leveraging our investigative research and international networks, MAI assists mid-market companies in executing corporate development and growth strategies.

Our Merchant Banking and M&A advisory services include:

  • Acquisition Prospecting: Mapping and identifying specific, off-market target companies that match the strategic growth criteria of our clients.
  • Purchaser Identification: Discreetly identifying and qualifying prospective institutional or strategic buyers for companies looking to transition ownership.
  • Deal Facilitation: Assisting in the valuation, structural negotiations, and overall management of transaction processes.

Strategic Partnerships & Joint Ventures

Expanding globally carries significant operational risk. MAI acts as a trusted facilitator to help firms identify and align with localized strategic partners.

Our partnership selection services include:

  • Joint Venture Sourcing: Locating complementary organizations with shared interests to establish secure global joint ventures.
  • Distributor & Representative Screening: Sourcing, screening, and recommending international distributors, sales agents, or legal representatives.
  • Market Access Advisory: Assisting in navigation, regulatory context, and setup options in the US, Latin America, Europe, and Asia.
