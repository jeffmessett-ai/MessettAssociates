Case Study: Executive Headhunting

The Challenge: A Cross-Border Leadership Void

A multi-national consumer products conglomerate needed to identify and recruit a **General Manager / VP of Operations** for their expanding Latin American division, headquartered in Miami with significant manufacturing sites in Brazil and Mexico.

The candidate needed to be completely trilingual (English, Spanish, and Portuguese), possess extensive supply chain knowledge in trade zones, and have a proven history of managing operations exceeding $100M in revenue. Previous contingent search attempts failed due to the specific combination of cultural experience and operational expertise required.

The MAI Approach

MAI was retained to execute the search. We mapped over 40 competitor consumer products manufacturers in the US, Brazil, and Mexico. We identified 18 potential candidates who fit the technical and linguistic criteria. After direct, discreet contact and deep face-to-face evaluations, we presented a shortlist of 3 pre-qualified candidates with detailed portfolios.

The Outcome

The search concluded within 75 days. The chosen candidate had:

  • Trilingual proficiency and a strong track record.
  • Over 15 years of operational leadership.
  • A history of improving production efficiency by 20% in previous roles.

"MAI's deep research and absolute confidentiality allowed us to target leaders who would never have applied through a job board. The placement has driven record division growth." - VP of Human Resources
