Welcome to Messett Associates, Inc.

Founded on the principles of trust, deep investigative research, and strategic placement, MAI acts as a confidential advisor to corporations seeking to fill critical leadership gaps or expand operations across international borders.

Unlike standard contingent recruiting agencies, MAI operates exclusively on a retained, consultative basis. This enables us to dedicate comprehensive resources to every search, ensuring we locate top-tier executives who are often not actively seeking new employment.

In addition to leadership identification, our corporate advisory practice guides organizations through the complexities of cross-border partnerships, joint ventures, and mergers and acquisitions (M&A).

The Retainer Advantage

Retained executive search is the method of choice for critical, high-salary positions (typically exceeding $150,000). It ensures absolute alignment between the firm's interests and your long-term success.

  • Dedicated research and sourcing team
  • Guaranteed project commitment
  • Rigorous pre-qualification & assessments
  • Strict confidentiality throughout the process
